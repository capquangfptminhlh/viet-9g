## Official source / proposed-fact register

- **F01** — [Viettel: USSD after the 2G switch-off](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/cap-nhat-phan-mem-va-thiet-bi-ho-tro-ussd-khong-lo-gian-doan-dich-vu-khi-tat-song-2g/14894320) (2026-07-03). Proposed fact: USSD commands depend on device/software support; My Viettel/Tammi and SMS are alternatives; `TK` to `191` checks account; `<package name>` to `191` is a registration channel; call `198` for support.
- **F02** — [Viettel: checking a data package's expiry](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/huong-dan-kiem-tra-ngay-het-han-cua-goi-cuoc-data-viettel/10455428). Proposed fact: `KTTK` to `191` returns the package name, included data, and expiry; My Viettel's **Thông tin lưu lượng** displays the package and expiry. It also says some packages auto-renew when the cycle ends and balance is sufficient, while others do not.
- **F03** — [Viettel: prepaid package guide](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/cap-nhat-2025-cach-dang-ky-goi-cuoc-tra-truoc-viettel-gia-re-moi-nhat/17031500). Proposed fact: to stop renewal, Viettel instructs `HUY <package name>` to `191`; wait for the system confirmation. My Viettel, `*098#`, and Hub are listed channels.
- **F04** — [Viettel: FB7/FB30 guidance](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/luot-facebook-khong-gioi-han-data-cung-viettel-voi-goi-cuoc-moi-fb7-va-fb30/17165624). Proposed fact: on these named packages, `HUY <package>` stops renewal and `HUYDATA <package>` cancels the package; they auto-renew at the end of their 7- or 30-day cycles. Do not generalize the benefit-retention behavior beyond the named package without current confirmation.
- **F05** — [Viettel: 2025 postpaid package guide](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/cap-nhat-2025-tong-hop-cac-goi-cuoc-tra-sau-viettel-gia-re-toc-do-cao-moi-nhat/16969792). Proposed fact: the guide lists `HUY <package name>` to `191`, cancellation in My Viettel, and calling `198` as cancellation-support channels; `KTTK` to `191` checks data allowance.
- **F06** — [Viettel: YT7 cancellation and checking](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/diem-danh-cac-goi-cuoc-data-viettel-xem-youtube-khong-gioi-han/10454296). Proposed fact: for the named YT7 package, `KTTK` to `191` checks remaining allowance; `HUY YT7` stops renewal and `HUYDATA YT7` cancels it. This is package-specific evidence only.
