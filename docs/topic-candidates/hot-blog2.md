## Evidence boundary

All proposed facts below come from Viettel-operated domains and must be re-opened immediately before drafting because device support, coverage, and product terms can change. `QUEUE` means the proposed angle is directly supported by an official source and has an existing, context-appropriate internal route; it is still subject to the normal fact recheck. `HOLD` means **do not draft** until the stated gap is resolved.

- **S1 — 5G settings/coverage:** [Viettel 5G coverage and iOS/Android setup](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/danh-sach-cac-quan-phu-song-5g-viettel-tai-tphcm-moi-nhat/17085448). It gives separate iOS and Android paths to the 5G option and says the device must be in a covered area to receive 5G.
- **S2 — eSIM 5G activation:** [eSIM 5G Viettel: activation guide](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/esim-5g-viettel-loi-ich-thiet-bi-ho-tro-va-cach-kich-hoat/10391888). It says a compatible, updated device and normally active subscriber are needed; the user receives a QR/configuration then adds a mobile plan and scans it.
- **S3 — eSIM hotspot:** [Can eSIM share Wi‑Fi?](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/giai-dap-esim-co-phat-wi-fi-duoc-khong-cach-chia-se-mang-don-gian/10594300). It states eSIM can provide 4G/5G data and can share the connection.
- **S4 — TV360 mobile-data policy:** [TV360 data policy from 05 Dec 2025](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/cap-nhat-chinh-sach-mien-phi-luu-luong-data-khi-truy-cap-tv360-tren-mang-di-dong-viettel/10261324). It distinguishes paid TV360 groups (zero-rated mobile data) from Free/Basic, whose usage is charged against the mobile-data package.
- **S5 — USSD over 4G/5G:** [USSD support after 2G shutdown](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/cap-nhat-phan-mem-va-thiet-bi-ho-tro-ussd-khong-lo-gian-doan-dich-vu-khi-tat-song-2g/14894320). It gives the VoLTE/IMS and `*101#`/`*098#` checks, and says iOS support example is iOS 18.1+.
- **S6 — overseas APN/VPN:** [No data in Japan: 4G/5G and roaming guide](https://www.vietteltelecom.vn/index.php/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/di-nhat-ban-khong-co-mang-huong-dan-bat-4g5g-va-roaming-viettel-chi-tiet/16239800). It cites APN `v-internet` and checking VPN as relevant device checks in that scenario.
- **S7 — 5G package inclusions:** [Viettel prepaid-package update](https://098.viettel.vn/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/cap-nhat-2025-cach-dang-ky-goi-cuoc-tra-truoc-viettel-gia-re-moi-nhat/17031500). It lists TV360 Basic with 5G135, 5G150, 5G160B, and 5G180B; this is used only to validate the relevance of each existing package route, not to make price or eligibility claims.
