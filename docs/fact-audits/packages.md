## Comparison guardrails

- The source is evidence for the listed attributes only; it does not show that every package is available to every SIM.
- Do not say “unlimited” for the social allowances: MXH120 and MXH150 explicitly include the “up to 50GB/usage guide” qualification.
- Comparisons may state arithmetic differences among the listed price, daily data, monthly data, and voice allowances. They must not claim one package is universally “best” or guarantee performance.
